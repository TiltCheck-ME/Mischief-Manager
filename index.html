Mischief Manager

Build awareness around impulse spending—triggers, cooldowns, and patterns—without shame or “just stop” lectures. This project also explores an ethical ad filter: user-controlled sensitivity and clear policy so harmful ads are easier to avoid or document.

View on GitHub Ethical ad filter docs

Who it’s for

Many people—especially with ADHD, bipolar disorder, or similar—experience extra friction around “add to cart” moments. Mischief Manager is being built as a habit and reflection tool: log urges, name triggers, use cooldowns, and see patterns over time. It does not diagnose mental health conditions or replace professional care.

What we’re building

  • Impulse & trigger awareness

    Track spending episodes and what led to them—mood, context, time of day—so you can spot repeat patterns.

  • Cooldowns & quick actions

    Short pauses and gentle prompts when you feel the pull to spend, so decisions are less automatic.

  • Insights (prototype)

    Dashboard-style views as the app matures; today’s prototype is best for demos—see the repo for current scope.

  • Ethical ad filter (architecture)

    Taxonomy, policy JSON, and evidence-bundle ideas to reduce exposure to high-pressure ads and support accountability—paired with optional URL-safety signals where consent allows.

Degen edition (positioning)

The same awareness-first philosophy can speak to people who gamble: reflect on tilt, “rinse” cycles, and bet sizing—you stay in control; the tool doesn’t lock accounts or replace regulated resources.
